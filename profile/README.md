### 🌍 Who We Are

FOTUR is a **web & automation studio** founded by developers who believe technology should be both **beautiful and functional**.  
We build modern websites, Telegram bots, and AI assistants that help businesses grow faster and work smarter.

> 💡 *"From landing pages to neural-powered systems — we turn ideas into living products."*

---

### 🧩 What We Do

#### 💻 Web Development  
Custom websites, landing pages, and web applications with unique design and pixel-perfect code.

#### 🤖 Telegram Bots  
Smart automation — from booking systems to full customer support bots and AI-integrated chat assistants.

#### 🧠 AI & Automation  
Integrating OpenAI, NVIDIA LLMs, and Yandex Cloud to create tools that think, analyze, and adapt.

#### 🎨 UI/UX Design  
We combine modern design systems, strong visual identity, and UX analytics to create products that convert.
